\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

We present EasyOPD, a method-oriented framework for integrating on-policy distillation methods with a shared verl execution backend. It separates user-side configuration, method-specific supervision construction, and distributed rollout and optimization. Through case studies in cross-tokenizer OPD, on-policy self-distillation, and step-wise OPD, we show that the evaluated methods retain their distinct supervision requirements within the same implementation structure. Rather than replacing verl's execution infrastructure, EasyOPD provides method-local extension boundaries and reproducible invocation on top of it, and we release it with runnable configurations, documentation, a demonstration package, and a video. Future work will expand method coverage and strengthen diagnostics and system-level evaluation.
