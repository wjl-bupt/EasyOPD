\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

We present EasyOPD, a method-oriented framework for integrating on-policy distillation methods with a shared verl execution backend. EasyOPD separates user-side configuration, method-specific supervision construction, and distributed rollout and optimization. Through case studies in cross-tokenizer OPD, on-policy self-distillation, and step-wise OPD, we show that the evaluated methods can retain their distinct supervision requirements within the same implementation structure. EasyOPD does not replace verl's execution infrastructure; rather, it provides method-local extension boundaries and reproducible invocation on top of it. We release the framework with runnable configurations, documentation, an installable demonstration package, and a demonstration video. Future work will expand the released method coverage and strengthen diagnostics and system-level evaluation.
